MY MOST HATED MONSTER

Omega Planetes

Omega Planetes, introduced in the Final Fantasy XIV Collaboration (Title Update 3) of Monster Hunter Wilds, is a highly mechanical, four-legged boss featuring immense durability, area-denial flames, and devastating laser attacks. It requires strategic team coordination to manage aggro, break legs, and use shielding mechanics to survive its deadly "Delta Attack".
