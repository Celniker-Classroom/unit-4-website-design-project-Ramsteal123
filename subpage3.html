List Of the Top 3 Best Monsters In Monster Hunter World

  • Nergigante
  • Fatalis

    • Descriptions

    Deviljho

    Deviljho, the nomadic "Violent Wyvern," is a volatile Brute Wyvern added to Monster Hunter: World as free DLC. Known for its extreme hunger, it disrupts ecosystems, appears in any high-rank locale, and uses devastating Dragon-element breath.

    Shara Ishvalda

    Shara Ishvalda, the "Old Everwyrm," is the primary antagonist of Monster Hunter World: Iceborne, capable of manipulating the earth through seismic vibrations generated by its unique wing-arms. This Elder Dragon initially fights in an armored, rocky shell, transitioning to a highly aggressive, slender true form that utilizes sand-based attacks and possesses unusual, forward-facing sensors.

    Nergigante

    Nergigante, the "Extinction Dragon" from Monster Hunter: World, is an aggressive Elder Dragon that acts as a natural regulator by hunting other Elder Dragons to feed on their bioenergy. It relies on rapid regeneration and hardened spikes, which it uses to deliver a massive, specialized dive-bomb attack.
