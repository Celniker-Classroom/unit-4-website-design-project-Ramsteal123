List Of the Top 3 Best Monsters In Monster Hunter Wilds

    • Descriptions

    Arkveld

    Arkveld, known as the "White Wraith and main villian in MHWilds," is a central, story-driven Flying Wyvern in Monster Hunter Wilds featuring unique, chain-like wing appendages that absorb environmental energy to fuel Dragon-element attacks. It also caused havoc to the whole continent destroying many important locations and killing many monsters.

    Zosia

    Zoh Shia is described in official lore as an ancient, uncontrollable bioengineered construct and high-threat Elder Dragon-like creature created by the extinct Wyveria civilization. Featuring specialized crystallized Wylk armor, this endgame threat feeds on the "Dragontorch" and is known for regenerating via "budding cocoons". When being attacked Its protection layer gets destroyed. but seconds later it grows the protection layer back.

    Rey Dau

    Rey Dau is the apex predator of the Windward Plains in Monster Hunter Wilds, acting as a living lightning rod that charges itself during Sandtide weather events. This bipedal Flying Wyvern uses its unique wing structure to slice through enemies, bladed protrusions to crash into them, and horn-based "railgun" mechanics to deliver electric-charged attacks, earning it the nickname "Destruction's Knell". This creature is one of the main rivals to arkveld
